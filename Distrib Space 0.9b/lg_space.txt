SPACE Plugin version 0.9b Written by Pascal Dallongeville (flemteam)

This plugin supports severals view mode (windowed, fullscreen,
docked and background) and is very customisable, enabled you to make
many preset of your choice (like AVS).

Dynamic Transform, customisable effects, preset support, good speed,
lot of visualisation modes, ... Try It !!

Strictly Minimum required configuration:
- 486 DX
- 16 Mo RAM
- a mouse
- 2D video card with directdraw and 16 bpp support (almost have)

... but with this you can get maybe one frame per minuts :(

Minimum recommended:
- MMX Processor
- 32 Mo of RAM
- mouse
- fast 2D video card for windowed mode

Really recommended:
- Celeron, PII 450 Mhz or better (to get 60 FPS+ in 400*300 FS "Vision Life")
- 64 Mo RAM
- TNT or better

I recommend use this plugin with teknos, trance, dance or dream...
and personally I think it looks best at at least 30 FPS
up to 60 FPS (depending your feeling).
