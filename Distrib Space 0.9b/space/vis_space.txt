STARTING SPACE
-------------
Run Winamp, open up the preferences (CTRL+P), go to the Plugins/Visualization
section, and select Space 0.9 as the visualization plug-in. Hit start. 

WHAT IS SPACE ?
---------------
SPACE is a plugin between AVS and GEISS.
You can choose yours effects, transformations, rendering and save
your presets exactly like in AVS. But all effects can be choosing
randomly by the plugin like in GEISS.
This let you create a lot of differents cools presets of your choice.
Once the plugin is started you can acces on a popup menu by right
cliking on the window area of the plugin. This menu let you customise
severals options.
SPACE has severals advantages:
- You have severals display modes (window, fullscreen, docks and BACKGROUND)
- You can change the resolution of the render independently of
your window size (for windowed mode), for instance: you can have
a 800X600 window with a 320X200 rendered effects for better performance
(but this need a fast video card for streched blit).
- Mirror effects that speed up renderings
- You can set all your preferences by a popup menu

CONFIGURATION
-------------
From the popup menu you can:
-Choose your display mode (Windowed, fullscreen, BACKGROUND ...)
-Choose your resolution (in windowed and docked mode only)
-Choose your speed (from slow to realtime)
-Add, Remove, randomly choosing effects (Waveform, cerclewave, ...)
-Choose your render (fade, clearscreen, fade & blur, blur, nothing ..)
-Choose your transformation (enabled, disabled, randomly, ...)
-Choose your mirror mode
-Save, load and load randomly presets

HOTKEYS
-------
* Any winamp key for control winamp
* ESCAPE to quit plugin (fullscreen and dock mode)
* F4 display FPS
* Rightclick for acces to the menu
(in top-left desktop corner for BACKGROUND mode, see NOTES section)

SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS
-------------------
you must have a directdraw compatible video card with a less directX 4.0
and 16 bpp support with winamp installed on your computer.
Older version (under 0.9) should works with directX 3.0

Strictly Minimum required configuration:
- 486 DX
- 24 Mo RAM
- 2D video card with directdraw support
... but with this you can get maybe one frame per minuts :(

Minimum recommended:
- MMX Processor (run about 60 FPS on K6-2 300 320*200 "Vision Life" preset)
- 32 Mo of RAM
- fast 2D video card for windowed mode

Really recommended:
- PII 450 Mhz or better (to get 60 FPS+ in 400*300 FS "Vision Life" preset)
- 64 Mo RAM
- TNT or better

I get about 65 fps with "Vision Life" Preset (slowest) on
my celeron300@464, TNT2 M64 with 64 Mo RAM in 400*300 fullscreen resolution
and 32 fps in 1024*768 with "Kalei Trip" preset (very cool).

TIPS
----
-> higher windows size means more video card power
-> Higher resolution need more CPU power
-> Fullscreen and background are the fastest modes (no big
difference if you have a fast 2D video card (like TNT2).

* I recommend use this plugin with teknos, trance, dance, hard or dream
musics because is very depending of bass and beat you hear in the
music (if music hasn't got any bass, SPACE won't react a lot) and personally
I think it look best between 30 and 60 FPS.
You should try thz background mode with the skin for winamp and theme for window
(you need the support of themes for window to try it) located in the 
/winamp/plugin/space directory, have fun !!

If your computer is too slow, you can try speed up things by:
- choosing AUTO/2 resolution (for windowed mode)
- lower resolution fullscreen mode or 160*100 in background mode
if your video can support it and if your computer is very slow..
- choosing mirror mode (vertical the slowest, double is fastest),
- choosing normal or fast blur instead of slow 
- drop blur rendering ... (and choose fadeout instead)
- Change Computer ;) 
- Somes presets are a lot faster than others: "Fast graph" is very fast, 
 "Bass Trip" is slow because "Fast Grpah" do not use Blur nor transformation
 unlike "Bass Trip".

-If you notice tearing with Space Plugin in fullscreen, try to increase
the refresh rate of directdraw.
For that , go in the "program files\directx\setup" directory an choose
"complementary help" section (the last) and click on the "change" button
(below "msinfo" button), then check the "change by the value" radio button
and fill the edit box to set the new value.
!!!!! ->Be carefull !!
You must set a value than your monitor should support (I have
set it to 120 Hz because my monitor could support 140 Hz in 1024*768 and
I don't use directdraw to resolution higher than 1024*768 in my plugin)
the default value is 60 Hz, my old 15'' works at 87 Hz in 1024*768.
If you set this value at 100 and that your monitor doesn't support higher
than 640*480 at 100 hz, then you won't be able to see higher screen than
640*480 in directdraw.
However for disabled most tearing, I recommend to set this value at 100 Hz, but
don't set this value if you aren't sure your monitor can support it.

-You can decrease tearing too by changing the speed of the plugin, try to adjust
to a value  that seems without (or with few) tearing.

FAQ
---
- Why I can't change option in background mode ?

When Space is in background mode, his window is located at the top left corner
of your desktop and you should keep in mind that winamp window mustn't be 
minimized for acces to space window. This is a little window of 10 * 10 pix
and if you want change option of Space you should right click on it. If you
press F4 and fps doesn't appears, this mean that space window hasn't focus, click
on the top left corner of our desktop (even if you don't see anything, there is
the space window) for give focus to the window and press F4.

- Why I get strange colors ?

Have you tried to check (uncheck) mode555 option in the Display Mode Menu ?
Sometimes in background mode the colors can be wrong because your video
card doesn't support the RGB or YUV overlay function. I haven't idea to fix this 
problem without a big loss of speed... If you have, then say me. Thanx

- Why Space is slower than Geiss ?

Because you use Geiss in 8 bit mode and 8 bit mode is a lot faster than 16 bit mode,
and I should reconize than Geiss is very optimised (I would like to know how is
their map transformation procedure ;) ... ).
However 16 bit mode give the possibility to display more color with blur and fadeout
and it is supported by almost video card (this isn't the case of 24 or 32bpp).
But I have noticed than Geiss could be slower than Space (even in 8 bpp) on slower
computer like K6-233, I don't know why ...

- Why resolution "AUTO/2" is slower than "AUTO" which calculate 4 more times data ?

Because resolution different than "AUTO" use the Streched blit functionality and
some video card haven't got this capabilities (Sis, Cirrus, ...). In this case
use only "AUTO" resolution for better performance.

- When I run the plugin in FULLSCREEN MODE, i get a messagebox saying:
'SetCooperativeLevel DDSCL_EXCLUSIVE|DDSCL_FULLSCREEN: DDERR_HWNDALREADYSET' ?

Make sure you're not using the DIRECTSOUND output plugin underneath Winamp... 
you have to use the WAVEOUT output plugin.

- I have only few resolution than appear for the FULLSCREEN MODE, and I am sure my
video card support more video mode.

Be sure that directdraw is correctly installed, reinstall directx to make sure.
note that you need directX 4 in order Space works (since version 0.9)
